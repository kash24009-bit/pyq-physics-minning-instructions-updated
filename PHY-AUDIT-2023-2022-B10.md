**Findings**
1. **2023-5521 Q34 and Q35 (and their shuffles in 55/2/2 and 55/2/3): the choice is stored with the wrong scope.** The paper prints "(a) passage, (i), (ii), (iii) ... OR (b) ...", and the rows store (b) as the alternative to the whole case. The 55/2 scheme's own split (p14-15) gives (b) 2 marks, "(iii) 1+1 OR (b) 1+1", so (b) replaces (iii) only, as in every other 2023 paper. Statistics and mocks would read these two cases wrong. Q-101 raised it at S3 and the lint flagged it at S5; neither was settled on the page.
2. **2023-5551: four rows hold a part's printed mark on the last of the rows the part was split into** (Q31A-i-d2 3, Q31B-i-ii 2, Q31B-ii-ii 3, Q33A-i-d2 3), so each of them shows fewer marks of lines than its mark. The 275 other parts in the vault split into -d rows (2022 to 2027) leave marks_part blank on their split rows, and the lint finds no other case. Q-202 queued it.
3. **2023-5551-Q16:** the assertion-reason key line is stored as step kind "other", not "key".
4. **Queue titles cut short in PROGRESS "Open":** Q-090 reads "K", Q-186 "u_e", Q-217 "m". The full items in QQUEUE are fine; only the summary line is cut.

## Usage: what the measurements say
- One year now costs about 5 sessions against 15 for 2026 and 12 to 15 for 2025, at the same accuracy. The cut came from the lean boot, readers that read only the rule card, half-page bands, the sibling worksheet and no double-read.
- The 2023 baseline (PHY-USAGE-BASELINE) shows where the rest goes: cache writes 49 %, output 31 %, cache reads 21 %. Images are the largest part of what is written: the 2023 intake alone opened 74 bands (about 154,000 image tokens) to read thirty page ones.
- What can still be cut without lowering the standard is listed in PHY-09 §4; the biggest saving left is in pattern sorting itself (one sheet per chapter instead of one command per row), which PHY-10 builds in.

## Her questions
- **Run 2022 and 2023 side by side?** No, and it is no longer needed: both years' papers and schemes are already in the vault. Two sessions at once would each restore the same vault and save their own copy; the newer one wins and the other session's work is silently lost (PHY-03 W-14). One session at a time.
- **Fable for pattern sorting?** Not needed. Pattern sorting reads text only; its quality comes from the procedure (a calibrated granularity test, the whole chapter in view, repeats and shuffles handled by script, and the checks), and this project measured no accuracy gain from a heavier setting (PHY-AUDIT-2026-B02: max against xhigh). Fable also uses her limits faster. Opus 5.5, as for all the mining.
- **Was pattern sorting planned from the start?** Yes. It is deliverable 2 in the project description and stage S6 of every year in CI§5. Chapters 01 and 02 of 2026 were sorted in Batch 2 as a pilot (181 rows), which raised the granularity question (Q-018). On 2 October she moved S6 to the closing sweep so the printed documents came first (B3-7).
