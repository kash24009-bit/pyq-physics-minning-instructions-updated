## A · Truth (every unit)
1. Every number, unit, sign, exponent, symbol, option, option letter, figure value, direction and half mark comes from the page image read at full resolution: on a born-digital page (its text layer matches the page) a 180 dpi half-page band (B3); on scans, handwriting, figure labels, plate lengths, arrow, diode and current directions, and any doubt, a 300-400 dpi crop (B4). Never from a shrunk whole page, the text layer alone, OCR or a script. The text layer only cross-checks digits; a conflict gets a 400 dpi crop. Known text-layer damage: 10^8 as "108", 30° as "300", hν as "h", ¹⁶₈O as "168O", B₁/B₂ as "B and B", dropped decimal points ("38 eV" for 3.8 eV), 1.6 µA as "1.6 mA". [her decision 2026-10-19b; CI§2.2, R§3, P§6.1]
2. Never invent. Unreadable at 400 dpi: `UNK` in the field, an UNREADABLE row (`problem` LOWRES), a note. [CI§2.1, P§6.5]
3. An extractor never writes a row from memory of another paper: it compares with the rows on disk. A blind checker does the opposite: it never opens the vault's tables, part files, sealed files or any earlier reading of its pages; its brief says what it may open. [CLAUDE §8, R§7]
4. A misprint is written as printed with `(sic)`, the correct physics computed in Python and put in the note (or in the line, C9). [CI§7, R§5]
5. Marks only as printed; never split, move or invent a mark. Halves as decimals (0.5, 1.5), never 1/2. [CI§2.6, R§5]
6. Every row carries its file_id, source_page and `batch` = `<N>`; ids as R§4.2. Write part files to `V/work/<unit>__<TABLE>.csv` with Python's `csv.DictWriter`; the header is the first line of the vault's own table (`head -1 V/l1/<TABLE>.csv`), same column order. Never write CONSTANTS outside an S2a unit. [R§4.1]
7. Do not touch git, do not commit, do not edit anything in `V/l1`, `V/j`, `STATE.json`, `PROGRESS.md`, `QQUEUE.md` or `notes/`. Your drafts go to `/home/claude/render/<unit>/NOTES-DRAFT.md` in the E2 format; Claude's gate step files them. [CLAUDE §8]
8. Never open the source of `pyqkit.py`, `phy_gate.py` or `phy_code_env.py`. [CLAUDE §8]
9. **Image sizes.** Every image you open stays within 1568 px on its long side and under 450 KB. [her decision 2026-10-21; Y§8]

A warning never blocks CHECK PASS. Settle each on the page image: fix the part file, or keep the row and give the reason in your return. [gate 2026-10-21; Y§4, Y§5]

## B · Reading pages with few steps
1. Open documents by file_id and always pass `--vault V`: `K pages --vault V <fid>` (page table), `K pages --vault V <fid> --text <p>` (text layer of one page), `K render --vault V <fid> <pages> --dpi 200 --crop --out /home/claude/render/<unit>/pages` (whole pages; `<pages>` as `5`, `5-9`, `5,7,9`). Crops: `K render --vault V <fid> <p> --dpi 400 --region x0,y0,x1,y1 --out /home/claude/render/<unit>/<label>`; region in page fractions of the full page; every crop in its own folder (the kit names files `p<page>.png`). A `--crop` render is trimmed to the content, so positions read off it are not page fractions. Render the whole pages you will place crops from once, without `--crop` (positions are then page fractions), or convert from the trimmed offsets. [R§3; B06 readers]
2. Render each page you need once, all in one Bash call; never render a page twice. [Y§8]
3. **Bands.** In one Bash call, render the unit's pages once at 180 dpi without `--crop` and cut each page into a top and a bottom band with a 4% overlap, one file per band: `python3 T/phy_bands.py --fid <fid> --pages <a-b> --out /home/claude/render/<unit>/bands` does both and prints only the band list. At 180 dpi a letter page is 1530 px wide and an A4 page about 1490 px, within A9's limit. Read the bands in page order in one or two batches of parallel Reads, and write that batch's rows right after it. [her decision 2026-10-19b]
4. **Crops only** for doubts, C2 candidates, figures, directions and scans: tight 400 dpi crops of the line or value (small crops cost little), all made in one Bash call, packed with `python3 T/phy_tile.py --out /home/claude/render/<unit>/sheets/<name> --dpi 400 <crop pngs>`, read in one turn. Scans: 300 dpi tiles within the limit (at most 0.6 x 0.42 of the page). Place regions as page fractions (the bands are uncropped pages, so a band position converts directly: band top covers 0-0.52, bottom 0.48-1 of the page height). [her decision 2026-10-19b]
5. Write part files in a few goes (one script per page band or per section), not row by row; rewrite the whole file with DictWriter each time. [B06]
6. Bilingual papers: English pages only. Hindi and English pages alternate, and the kit's page table calls Hindi pages "en" because their text layer is not Unicode, so a Devanagari count misses them: take the worksheet's list of English pages where it gives one, and decide any other page from its image. A Hindi page is the backup for a damaged English one (`read_from = hi-translation`). Scans without a text layer: no dual-read; say so in the note and use 300 to 400 dpi crops. [R§3, CONV B01, B05; her decision 2026-10-21]
7. **Step budget.** A scheme unit takes about 15 steps: card and worksheet together; one render call; bands in one or two batches, each followed by one write; one script for the physics, the sums and the FORMULAE result rows; the second look; one crop call and one parallel read for every doubt; `G check`; return. Text steps come before any image is opened; never re-open an image already in view; tool output stays short (counts and failures, never whole files). Paper units follow the same order. [her decision 2026-10-19b]

## C · S5 scheme units (STEP-AWARDS)
1. **Scope.** One unit = one set's section of the scheme, under the paper_id of that set (55/1/2 → `2024-5512`); read only that section's pages; never apply a scheme to another paper_id. Lines only for this paper's `rel = origin` rows; shuffle rows inherit their origin's lines and get none. [R§10, Y§5, W-1]
2. **Shuffles printed differently.** If this scheme prints a different key, value or award for a shuffled question than the origin's scheme, write no line; report it with its page (the worksheet flags candidates from the text layer; confirm each on the image). [R§10; Q-015, Q-048]
3. **Mapping.** Map the scheme's answers to the rows by content (numbering can differ), and check the mapping on the image. [R§10]
4. **One STEP-AWARDS row per printed scoring line, in printed order.** `award_id` `SA-<instance_id>-<nn>` (01, 02 … per instance); `instance_id`; `paper_id`; `file_id` = the scheme file; `step_no` 1, 2 …; `step_text` = what the line awards, normalised (D11), including the printed result with its unit; `marks` as printed (0.5, 1, 1.5); `step_kind` one of formula, substitution, result, unit, diagram, label, reason, definition, key, other; `mandatory` `y` only where the scheme says the line is compulsory, else `n`; `alt_accepted` (C6); `provenance` `PRINTED`; `source_page`; `batch`. [R§10, P§7]
   Template: `SA-2024-5511-Q22-a-01, 2024-5511-Q22-a, 2024-5511, FL-f9e28e6de8, 1, "a) phi_L = vec(E_L).vec(A) = -[500 x 0.1] x [(0.1)^2] = -0.5 N m^2 C^-1", 0.5, substitution, n, , PRINTED, 6, 5`
5. **MCQ and assertion-reason:** one `key` row, step_text the printed letter only, e.g. `(C)`, marks 1. Two printed keys: `(A) / (C)`, marks 1. An OR-alternative key with no mark printed beside it takes the sub-part's 1. [CONV S5 B02, B03]
6. **Unmarked routes** ("Alternatively", "or any other correct method", "award full marks for any other relevant answer") go into `alt_accepted` of the line they replace, never as lines. An alternative printed with halves of its own also goes there, written "Alternatively (printed ½ + ½): …", so a part's lines still add up to its marks. [CONV S5 B02, 2025 examiner instructions; Y§5]
7. **Special awards.** "Award full marks for attempting", "full credit for mere attempt", "no option is correct", "award 1 mark to all students", "give full marks for any choice": one `other` line with the printed words and the printed mark (or the part's mark). [Y§5]
8. **Marks layout.**
   - A combined cell ("½ + ½" or "1 + ½ + ½" beside one line listing several items): one row per printed amount, the cell's text kept in each step_text with "(printed ½ + ½ in one cell)". [CONV S5 B02; SA-2024-5511-Q21-*]
   - **A half belongs to the line it is printed beside.** On a born-digital scheme the worksheet ends with the printed-marks list (`phy_marks.py`): every printed mark with the text printed on its line ("beside"), or the lines just above and below it ("between"), and a line marked `**` wherever the marks listed do not add up to a printed total. In the second look, compare every line's mark with that list. The list comes from the text layer, so the image decides; a `**` line means the list missed or misread a mark there. 2024-5531-Q31B-ii had its halves shifted by one line, with the right total (Q-078, fixed by FIX-7). [her decision 2026-10-21; Y§5]
   - A half printed between two parts' lines goes where the scheme's description box (the split printed at the head of the answer) puts it. [CONV S5 B02]
   - Box against line marks: the marks printed beside the lines decide; write the conflict in the step_text of the first line of each part and report it for the queue. [CONV S5 B02, Y§5; Q-014, Q-049, Q-057, Q-064]
   - A printed answer with no mark beside it (the part's lines then fall short): one line, marks 0, step_text noting "printed with no mark of its own"; report it. [CONV B03; Q-021]
   - The lines of each part add up to its printed marks (marks_part where printed, otherwise the question's lines to q_marks_printed). Check by script before the gate; the lint checks it again. [Y§5]
9. **Physics check.** Compute every numerical answer in Python. A slip: keep the printed text and append `(sic; <correct working and value>)` on the line and on the FORMULAE result row; report it. Rounding by the scheme: `(rounded in the scheme, not a slip; exact: …)`. A doubtful key: keep the printed letter; report it with the working. [Y§5, CONV B03; Q-063]
10. **Scheme symbols** that differ from the paper's are kept as printed in step_text; report the mapping. [CONV S5 B02]
11. **FORMULAE result rows** for numerical answers printed only in the scheme: `formula_id` `EQ-<pid>-<nnn>` continuing after the paper's last number (the worksheet gives the next free one); `instance_id`; `role` `result`; `formula` as printed, e.g. `T = T_o + 250 = 270 deg C or 543 K`; `quantities`; `units` (`-` for a pure number, `; ` between several); `source` `MS`; `source_page` the scheme page; `from_image` `y`; `batch`. One per numerical row whose question gives real data and whose scheme prints a final value. [R§10; EQ-2024-5511-077]
12. **Scheme file status.** Only the unit that finishes the last set of a scheme file writes `<pid>__ERRATA.csv`: file `l1/FILES.csv`, row_id the scheme file_id, field `status`, NEW → DONE, reason "scheme pass S5 read from <fid>: all sets done", evidence = the set-section headers and pages as read on the images; the id given in the brief. A Hindi-medium twin is set DONE in the same unit and never gets a unit of its own. [R§10, CONV Y2024 schemes; ER-B03-129]

## D · S3 and S4 paper units (INSTANCES, FIGURES, FORMULAE, CASES, BLUEPRINTS)
1. **One row per printed part label** ((I), (II), (A), (B), (a), (i) …), with the label as printed in `part`; printed sub-labels inside a part get their own rows (`-Q22A-b-i`). [CONV B0, B01; CI§7]
2. **Ids:** instance `<pid>-Q<nn><side>[-<part>…]` (two-digit question number, no dots); figure `F-<pid>-<nn>`; formula `EQ-<pid>-<nnn>`; case `CS-<pid>-Q<nn>`; choice pair `OR-<pid>-Q<nn>`. [R§4.2]
3. **Sides and choices.** Internal-choice alternatives take sides A and B, with `choice_pair_id` `OR-<pid>-Q<nn>` on both. A single-part alternative printed "(a) OR (b)" is sides A/B with no part; where each side has its own (a), (b), those are parts (`-Q22A-a`). A choice inside a case sub-part: untouched sub-parts keep side blank (`-Q29-I`); the alternatives take sides A/B with the sub-part label (`-Q29A-III`, `-Q29B-III`), one choice_pair_id, `extra` `choice_in_part=III`; BLUEPRINTS lists such questions under D. [CONV B01]
4. **Marks.** `q_marks_printed` on every row; `marks_part` only where a mark is printed beside that part; a marks cell shared by both sides of an OR gives marks_part only where the split is printed beside the part; marks taken from the instructions page (no mark printed, e.g. the 2024 55/5 sets; case totals) → `extra` `marks_from=…`; a printed split such as 2+1 mapped to labelled parts in order → `extra` `printed_split=…`. [R§5, CONV B03; Y2024 notes]
5. **Text fields.** `command_verb` = the printed demand words, `...` for the elided middle. `stem` = the complete printed text of the part with its printed sub-labels, normalised (D11). `givens` and `unknown` on every row that has quantities. A case study keeps its own convention: the passage goes into CASES as a two-sentence context, and each sub-part's givens list what that sub-part uses, with "as in the passage" for the rest (2024-5531-Q30-*). 6. **Options** on MCQ rows: `A) … | B) … | C) … | D) …`. Assertion-reason rows keep `options` blank, as every paper since Batch 1 does: the paper's first A-R row carries the printed option set as `extra` `ARSET=…`, and a copied row that is not the paper's first A-R row must not keep an ARSET (the lint warns). Options never go on any other row (the gate refuses them). [R§5, gate 2026-10-02, B5-1]
7. **Classification fields.** `q_type` one of MCQ, AR, DEF (define, state a law, unit, dimension), CON (explain, why, reason), PRD (how does X change, what happens if), NUM, DRV (derive, obtain, show that, prove), DGM (draw or label a diagram), GRF (plot, sketch, interpret a graph), CMP (compare, distinguish), DEV (device principle, construction, working), EXP (experiment-based). `chapter` 01–14, 15 for Communication Systems, X11 for Class 11 content; `topic_id` only from `V/j/SYLLABUS.csv`; `syllabus_status` IN, DELETED, VERIFY-DEL, X11 or UNK (deleted topics kept with their status). [R§5, Y§6]
