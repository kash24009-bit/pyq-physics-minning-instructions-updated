## 4 · Truth rules (the project's CI§2, verbatim)
1. **Never invent** a year, paper code, question number, mark, value, unit, option, figure detail, scoring step, answer or fact. Unknown is written `UNK`; something she must supply is written `ASKED`.
2. **Nothing mathematical or visual comes from a text layer alone.** Numbers, units, symbols, sub/superscripts, fractions, Greek letters, vectors, circuit values, diagrams, graphs, tables and half-marks come from the page image. On project-knowledge pages (bundles), use the dual-read: structure from the image, digits cross-checked against the page text; a conflict is flagged (P§6).
3. **Provenance on every row:** `file_id`, `source_page`, `q_no`, `batch`.
4. **Layer 1 is append-only.** Corrections go in ERRATA (old, new, reason, page evidence). Rows are never moved between papers or edited silently.
5. **Layer 2 is regenerated by `pyqkit`**, never edited by hand.
6. **Marks come only from the paper:** its printed marks and its own instructions page. When they conflict, record both and flag.
7. **Every number in any report or reply is computed by the kit** from the data, never typed.
8. **Facts about CBSE carry VERIFIED** (read in an official document) **or REPORTED** (news, analysis sites). Recalled archetypes live as DRAFT and never enter statistics until a real instance exists.
9. **Archetype codes are issued only in REGISTRY.csv.** They are never renumbered or reused. A merge uses status `ALIAS`; a split issues new codes and records the old one.
10. **Topics with status DELETED or VERIFY-DEL** are kept for the method layer and never appear in a mock.
11. **A marking scheme is never applied to a paper of another year or series** unless the steps are labelled `TRANSFERRED:<source>`.
12. **A failing check quarantines that paper, not the session.** Continue with the rest; re-read the paper next. Never fix a number without an ERRATA row and page evidence.
13. **Nothing is left silently.** Every open item is in PROGRESS.md under "Open" and in the year-close report.
14. **No blocker is ever aimed at her** (CI§8).

## 7 · The per-paper loop (S3/S4; S5 is the same with `--stage S5`, R§10)
1. The next paper is the first paper not yet marked done on the first unticked PROGRESS line of the current stage.
2. **Extract.** Start a fresh subagent with the extractor brief (R§6). It writes part files in `/home/claude/vault/work/` and runs `phy_gate.py check` until it passes. It never merges.
3. **Seal.** `python3 /home/claude/phy_gate.py skeleton <paper_id> --stage S4` writes the checker's input and moves the part files out of reach.
4. **Blind check.** Start a second, fresh subagent with the checker brief (R§7). It writes `<paper_id>__VERIFY.csv` from the page images alone.
5. **Settle.** `python3 /home/claude/phy_gate.py verify <paper_id> --stage S4`. For every mismatch, read a 300–400 dpi crop of that exact spot yourself, correct the part file if the extractor was wrong, and record the decision in `<paper_id>__ADJUDICATE.csv` (R§8). Repeat until verify exits 0.
6. **Judged text.** Observations go in the vault's `notes/Y<year>.md` (each backed by instance ids), new conventions in `CONVENTIONS.md`, open questions in `QQUEUE.md`.
7. **Commit.** `python3 /home/claude/phy_gate.py commit <paper_id> --stage S4`. This is the only door into the vault: it re-checks, merges, writes the status erratum and the PROGRESS line with computed counts, validates, packs, commits and pushes. Exit 0 means the paper is saved.
8. Only then open the next paper. One paper at a time, never two in parallel.
