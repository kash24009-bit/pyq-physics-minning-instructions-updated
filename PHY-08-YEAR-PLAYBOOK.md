## Y§5 · S5 schemes
R§10, plus the conventions Batches 2 to 6 settled (CONVENTIONS "S5 scheme pass"; rule card C):
- **Key rows:** an MCQ or assertion-reason answer is one `key` line, step_text the printed letter only, e.g. `(C)`, marks 1.
- **"Award full marks for attempting" or "no option is correct":** one `other` line with the printed words, not a key line (2026-5531-Q02, 2026-5553-Q03, 2026-5521-Q29-iii).
- **Combined mark cells** ("½ + ½" beside one line listing several items): one line per printed half.
- **A half belongs to the line it is printed beside.** On born-digital schemes the worksheet ends with `phy_marks.py`'s list: every printed mark with the text on its line, the lines around it where it sits between two, and a `**` line wherever the listed marks do not add up to a printed total. The reader compares every line's mark with it in the second look (card C8). 2024-5531-Q31B-ii had its halves shifted by one line with the right total, so no sum check could see it: a later reader queued it from a sibling scheme's page (Q-078), the year check's sample confirmed it, FIX-7 fixed it. Tested on all 20 set sections of the 2024 schemes (R§10): 2,715 marks listed, 649 of 667 printed totals matched by the list itself, and no question differing from the year-checked lines except where an OR or "Alternatively" explains it.
- **A line that runs over a page break** cites the page where its mark is printed (card C15; three 2024 lines aligned by ER-B06-018 to -020).
- **Unmarked routes** ("Alternatively", "award full marks for any other correct …"): in `alt_accepted` of the line they belong to; an alternative printed with halves of its own also goes there (card C6).
- **Scheme symbols** that differ from the paper's are kept as printed; the mapping goes in `notes/Y<year>.md`.
- **Split box against line marks:** the marks printed beside lines decide; the box decides only a half printed between two parts; a conflict becomes a Q-QUEUE item (2026: Q-014, Q-016).
- **Physics errors in a scheme:** compute every numerical answer in Python. Keep the printed text and append "(sic; <correct working and value>)", on the line and on the FORMULAE `result` row, plus a notes line. A doubtful key: the printed key is kept and queued with the working (2026: Q-015; 2024: Q-075, Q-077, Q-081, Q-082, Q-090).
- **Shuffles inherit:** lines only for the paper's `rel = origin` rows. Every part's lines add up to its printed marks. A shuffle that the scheme prints differently gets no line; it is reported (card C2).
- **"Award 1 mark to all students" / "give full marks for any choice":** one `other` line with the printed words (2025-5553-Q29-ii, Q-048; 2025C-55BS-Q29-iii, Q-037).
- **A description box against the line marks** (55/5/1 Q21, Q-049): the line marks are kept; the conflict is a Q-QUEUE item.
- **Two printed keys** for one MCQ (55/2/1 Q12): CONVENTIONS "Two printed keys (B03)". **A printed answer with no mark beside it** (SQP 2026-27): a marks-0 line noting "printed with no mark of its own".
- **One PDF for several sets** (2024, 2025): each set's section is its own S5 unit, the paper_id of that set. **A compartment scheme for set 1 only** (2025: 55/S/1): the other sets' new rows go to the DEFERRED "papers with no scheme" line.
- **Numerical results:** a FORMULAE `result` row (`source` MS) for every NUM row whose question gives real data and whose scheme prints a final value (card C11; on 2026-10-21, 551 of the 563 NUM rows with printed lines had one, and the other 12 have symbolic answers).
- In the same unit, ERRATA setting the scheme file DONE (and its Hindi-medium twin). Commit with `--progress "Y<year> S5 scheme <code> ("`.

## Y§6 · The detail standard
Measured on 2026's sibling papers' new rows, the trial (xhigh) wrote figure descriptions of about 200 characters, 1.86 formula rows per numerical or derivation row, and notes on 61 % of rows; the overnight run (max) wrote 143 characters, 1.38 and 35 %. Both were equally accurate on values. The lean 2024 scheme units were checked line by line against the scheme pages in the project chat: 58 of 58 lines exact, Section A keys 32 of 32 (CONVENTIONS "Lean reading, part 2"). Write every new row to that level:
- **stem:** the complete printed text of the part, its printed sub-labels included, normalised per P§6.3.
- **givens and unknown** on every row that has quantities; the numbers of a shared lead-in on every sub-part row (Q-094, her decision 2026-10-21); a case study's sub-parts as card D5.
- **FIGURES:** every element named with its value and position, and every direction: which side each cell's longer plate faces, which way each diode and arrow points, current, field and ray directions, a graph's axes, labels, intercepts and shape. `values` holds only numbers the figure prints; a measurement (pixels, a drawn angle, a fraction of an axis) goes in the description, marked "measured, not printed".
- **FORMULAE:** `given` for each printed relation; `asked` for every relation the demand needs (a derivation's chain, not just its end); `result` from the scheme at S5.
- **note:** on every new sibling row "resembles <id>: <what differs>" or "new question"; on any row, every reading decision (text layer overruled, (sic), choice layout).
- **Notation:** the project's ASCII forms (sqrt(2), r_1, lambda_a, microF, vec(E), i^, 3e8). A plain v printed is a plain v, never vec(v). Powers of ten as 1e-2, never 10^-2 (the lint warns).

## Y§8 · Saving usage without losing accuracy
What a unit costs, by the meter's weights (Y§1): thinking and every other output weigh most; then new content written to the cache (every image once, the start-up once per reader, a cold conversation all over again); re-reading the conversation weighs least. So:
- **Fewer steps.** Each step thinks. A scheme unit takes about 15 steps (card B7): text steps first, images in one or two batches with one write each, one script for the physics, sums and result rows, the second look against the images already open, one crop call for every doubt, the gate check, the return.
- **Images at full resolution, no larger than needed.** Born-digital pages: two 180 dpi half-page bands per page (about 2,000 visual tokens each; on the 2024 55/1 scheme 1530 × 1030 px and 111 KB on average, the largest 269 KB). Scans: never above their own resolution (`pdfimages -list`), saved as 8-bit grayscale (on the same pages grayscale halves the bytes: 51 KB a band). Tight 400 dpi crops only for doubts, figures, directions and scans; a crop of one value costs little.
- **Anthropic's documented limits (read 2026-10-21).** An image costs ⌈width/28⌉ × ⌈height/28⌉ visual tokens. Opus 5.5 is on the high-resolution tier: a long edge up to 2576 px and 4,784 visual tokens before it is downscaled. A request carrying more than 20 images (a reader's always does) is refused if any image passes 2000 px on a side. Claude Code resizes large images before sending, and from v2.1.196 re-encodes any image still over 500 KB as a lower-quality JPEG. The card's limits, 1568 px and 450 KB, keep a margin under all of these; `phy_measure.py`'s image check compares each image's token cost with its pixel size, so a shrunk image would show.
- **Not done, with the reason:** trimming white side margins saves about 8 % of band tokens (measured on the 2024 55/1 scheme) but makes band positions no longer page fractions, which the crop rule (card B4) relies on.
- **Calibration on every new file:** twelve values spread over the unit re-read at 400 dpi must match 12/12, or that file goes back to 400 dpi crops.
- **Lean readers:** `phy-reader` subagents carry only their own prompt and six tools; one fresh reader per unit; readers that start back to back share the start-up's cache.
- **A small main session:** two steps per unit, no images, tool output trimmed, reports of at most 12 lines; the lean boot (CLAUDE.md §6); a new session after every usage stop; no pull-request watching, check-ins or wake-ups.
- **Scripts for mechanical work:** worksheets locate answers, align siblings and list printed marks; the gate checks coverage, sums and the lint; confirmed repeats are written by script. The reader's thinking goes to reading the page.
- Don't open the source of `pyqkit.py`, `phy_gate.py` or `phy_code_env.py` to learn how they work; R§9 and this file are enough. A suspected kit bug is the only exception (R§11).
- Keep judged notes to 2–4 lines per paper in `notes/Y<year>.md`, each with instance ids.
